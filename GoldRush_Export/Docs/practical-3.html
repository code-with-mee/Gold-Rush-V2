GOLD RUSH · Practical 3 · Hard

Guard Tower

In the middle of the yard stands a vault: four walls with a front door and a back door. Inside, the Mushroom King guards three coins around his Home. The other coins hide behind corner walls and blocks, and a Slime walks outside. Take the GHOST power-up, and you have 6 seconds to sneak in, grab the gold and get out.

1Goal and values

Goal

Build a vault level with 9 coins (3 inside the vault), 1 Mushroom King with a Home marker, 1 Slime and a GHOST power-up.

You learn: a closed room with two exits, a Guard and its Home, tall and low cover, coins behind cover, a power-up as a tool.

What you need: Practical 2 (walls with rotation 90) and your project with the Phase 10 package. You start from its Level1.

A challenge. This practical has no screenshots. You work the same way as in Practicals 1 and 2: copy Level1, clear it out, drag kit pieces from Prefabs/Level and markers from Prefabs/Markers, then add the LevelDef. Stuck on a step? Look at the same step in Practical 2.

The LevelDef values (you type them in step 11):

parTime70 seconds (tune it in the play test)
lives2 (the default, you can leave it out)

2The map

This is your level from above. North (+Z) is up. One grid square is 2 m, like the floor tiles. The numbers on the coins are the CoinSpot numbers.

The dashed circle is where the King walks. The vault walls hide you from him until you stand in a door. The coins outside are each behind something, seen from the vault.

3Make the scene

  1. Open Scenes/Level1. File → Save As… → Assets/GoldRush/Scenes → name Level7 → Save.
  2. Check the title bar: it says Level7.
  3. Rename the root Level 1 - Training Yard to Level 7 - Guard Tower.
  4. In its Level Scene component, set Arena Half = 16.
  5. Save.

4Clear out

  1. Delete all 12 children of Static/Obstacles and Static/Structures/LCover.
  2. Delete the three FloorPads in Decor (one of them would lie inside the vault). Keep the FloorStrip lines.
  3. Delete Gameplay/PowerUps/PowerUp Magnet and Gameplay/Monsters/Slime 2.
  4. Keep Slime 1 and the 6 CoinSpots: you move them and add 3 more.

5Lamps

The 8 lamps stay where they are in Level1. They are not in the way of the new pieces.

Check one thing: lamp 6 at 14.4, 0, 0 and the red landmark at 11, 0, 0 leave a passage of about 2 m. That is OK for the hero.

6Build the vault and the cover

  1. Right-click Static → Create Empty. Name it Vault. Position 0, 0, 0.
  2. Build the vault first (pieces 1–13): pillars, then the walls between them. Then the outside cover (15–25).

The vault is 13 × 13 m. Its walls leave two doors, each 4 m wide: one in the south (X −2…2) and one in the west (Z −2…2). The King is big (radius 0.75 m), so doors must be wide.

Drag each prefab onto its parent in the Hierarchy and type the values. Scale stays 1, 1, 1.

7Coins

You need 9 CoinSpots. A CoinSpot is an empty marker: the game makes the coin 1 m above it.

  1. Open Gameplay/Coins. Keep the CoinSpots you have and move them to the new positions.
  2. Need more? Select a CoinSpot and press Ctrl+D / Cmd+D (or drag Prefabs/Markers/CoinSpot onto Gameplay/Coins). Rename it with the next number.
  3. Delete CoinSpots you do not need.

8Monsters

Monsters go into Gameplay/Monsters. To add one, drag a marker from Prefabs/Markers onto the group, type the position, and rename it. Delete the old Slimes you do not need.

NameMarkerPositionNote
Mushroom King 1Monster_MushroomKing (new)0, 0, 2Inside the vault. Home = KingHome (see below).
Slime 1Monster_Slime12, 0, -4Outside, east. Keep Slime 1 from Level1 and move it (you deleted Slime 2 in step 4).

Give the King a Home. A Guard stays within 4.5 m of its Home and gives up the chase 13 m from it.

  1. Right-click Gameplay/Monsters → Create Empty. Name it KingHome. Position 0, 0, 0 (the centre of the vault).
  2. Select Mushroom King 1. In Monster Spawn, drag KingHome from the Hierarchy into the Home field.
  3. In the Scene view you see a dashed circle (4.5 m) around KingHome and a line to the King. The circle must stay inside the vault.

9Power-up

A power-up is a PowerUpSpot marker (Phase 8). The game makes the orb above it at Play.

  1. Drag Prefabs/Markers/PowerUp_Ghost onto Gameplay/PowerUps.
  2. Rename it PowerUp Ghost. Position -9, 0, -3.
  3. Check its Power Up Spot: Type = Ghost.

The GHOST orb is 4 m from the back door. Plan: grab it, run in through the back door, take coins 1–3, and leave through the front door. 6 seconds is short: try it!

10Start, gate and NavMesh

  1. Select Gameplay/PlayerSpawn. Position 0, 0, -14.5, Rotation 0, 0, 0 (looks north, to the gate).
  2. Select Static/ExitGate. Position 0, 0, 16, Rotation 0, 0, 0.
  3. Select Static/Walls/PerimeterWall/GateBlocker. It stays at 0, 2.5, 17.25 (Box Collider 7.5, 5, 1). Do not change it.
  4. Select Static. In NavMesh Surface, click Bake. Look at the blue area: it must reach every coin and the gate.
  5. Save.

From the start you look straight at the vault's front door. The start moves to Z -14.5. The King's circle ends 10 m from it, and he sees only 9.5 m, so he can not see you at the start. Do not move the start closer.

11LevelDef and Build Profiles

  1. Open Assets/GoldRush/Scripts/Core/Data.cs. Find Levels.All.
  2. Add this line at the end of the list (after the last level you have):
new LevelDef { title = "Guard Tower", subtitle = "Sneak past the King", seed = 707, scene = "Level7", size = 32, coins = 9, patrollers = 1, guards = 1, powerUps = 1, enemySpeed = 1f, parTime = 70 },
  1. Save the file. Wait for Unity to compile. No red errors.
  2. File → Build Profiles → Scene List: drag in Scenes/Level7. Check it is ticked.

Not sure? Read Practicals, step 6 and 7.

12▶ Play test

  1. Open Level7 and press ▶ Play.
  2. Play the level to the end. Then play it 2 more times and write down your times.
  3. Open MainMenu → Play → LEVELS. Find the GUARD TOWER card.

▶ Play test: check these

  • The King walks around inside the vault and does not leave it, except to chase you.
  • Both doors let you (and the King) through.
  • When the King chases you and you run 13 m from KingHome, he turns back.
  • With GHOST active, the King walks past you.
  • Every coin outside is hidden from the vault doors.
  • The intro says GUARD TOWER and "Collect all 9 gold".
  • The gate opens after the last coin, and you win when you run through it.
  • No red errors in the Console.

The card: it shows GUARD TOWER, "Sneak past the King" and "9 GOLD TO FIND". It is locked until you win the level before it. With 6 or more levels the cards are in two rows (see Practicals, step 8).

Tune the par time: best time + 25 %, rounded to 5 s. Change parTime in Data.cs.

13Make it harder

Make it harder (pick one or two, test after each):
  • Add a second King: a Monster_MushroomKing marker at 11, 0, 12 with its own Home KingHome 2 at 11, 0, 12 (it guards coin 5).
  • Close the back door with a Wall_4m rot 90 at -6.5, 0, 0, and move the GHOST orb next to the front door.
  • Change the Slime into a Ghost: Monster Spawn → Type = Chaser.
  • Set enemySpeed = 1.1f and lives = 1: one hit and you lose.

14Self-check

Be honest. Better: let a friend play and fill it in for you.

Check1 point: OK2 points: good3 points: great
WorksThe level loads from the menu card. The gate opens after the last coin.All coins can be reached. No red errors in the Console. The King has a Home and stays in his circle.The par time and the coin number in Data.cs match the level.
FunThere is something to do on the way to every coin.The vault is exciting: you plan when to go in.You want to play it again to beat the par time.
FairNo monster can see you at the start.There are two ways out of the vault. The GHOST power-up is easy to find.A friend can win it on the 2nd or 3rd try, without your help.
Looks goodNothing floats or sinks into the floor.The vault is a clear landmark. Nothing overlaps or has gaps.The level has a clear theme. Lamps and colours point the way.

Give yourself 1–3 points per row. 8 points or more: well done. Less? Fix the lowest row first, then test again.

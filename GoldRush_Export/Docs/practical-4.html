GOLD RUSH · Practical 4 · Free design

Night Run

The final test. A bigger 36 × 36 m arena at night, with four corner buildings, a plaza with the Mushroom King, two Slimes and a Ghost near the exit. The MAGNET power-up lets you pull the King's coins from a safe distance. Lamps light the way in the dark. First you build the base level from the map, then you change it with your own idea.

1Goal and values

Goal

Build a 36 × 36 m night level with 12 coins, all 3 monster types (2 Slimes, 1 Ghost, 1 Mushroom King with Home), a MAGNET and 8 lamps. Then change at least 3 things to make it your own design.

You learn: a bigger arena, an off-centre gate, lights at night, combining monsters, using a power-up as a tool, designing on your own.

What you need: Practical 3 (Guard and Home) and Phase 10 (the lamps have a NightLight, the HUD has the moon button).

The LevelDef values (you type them in step 12):

FieldValue
title"Night Run"
subtitle"Gold under the moon"
scene"Level8"
size36 (36 × 36 m, Arena Half 18)
coins12
monsterspatrollers = 2 (Slimes) · chasers = 1 (Ghosts) · guards = 1 (Mushroom Kings)
powerUps1
enemySpeed1.08f
parTime100 seconds (tune it in the play test)
lives2 (the default, you can leave it out)
nighttrue

2The map

This is your level from above. North (+Z) is up. One grid square is 2 m, like the floor tiles. The numbers on the coins are the CoinSpot numbers.

The safe way goes round the outside (alleys), the fast way goes through the plaza. The Ghost guards the exit, so the last run is the hardest part.

3Make the scene

  1. Open Scenes/Level1. File → Save As… → Assets/GoldRush/Scenes → name Level8 → Save.
  2. Check the title bar: it says Level8.
  3. Rename the root Level 1 - Training Yard to Level 8 - Night Run.
  4. In its Level Scene component, set Arena Half = 18.
  5. Save.

4Clear out

  1. Delete all 17 children of Static/Obstacles.
  2. Keep PowerUp Magnet, both Slimes and the 6 CoinSpots: you move them and add more.

5Make the arena bigger

The arena grows from 32 to 36 m. 36 m is not "8 m pieces + an 8 m gap in the middle", so the gate moves 2 m west (X -2). Change the wall groups from Level1 like this:

  1. WallSouth: Position 0, 0, -18.5. Move its four Wall_8m to X -14, -6, 2, 10. Add one Wall_4m at X 16.
  2. WallEast (18.5, 0, 0) and WallWest (-18.5, 0, 0), Rotation 0, 90, 0: the same children as WallSouth.
  3. WallNorth: Position 0, 0, 18.5. Children: Wall_8m at -14, Wall_4m at -8, then the gap, then Wall_8m at 6 and 14. (Delete the Wall_4m at X 6 and add a Wall_8m.)
  4. Corners: pillars at ±18.5, 0, ±18.5.

6Lamps

At night the lamps are the main light (their NightLight from Phase 10 turns them on). Place the 8 lamps so they show the way:

10Power-up

A power-up is a PowerUpSpot marker with the matching prefab inside (like in Phase 8).

  1. Right-click Gameplay/PowerUps → Create Empty. Name it PowerUp Magnet. Position 15, 0, 1.
  2. Add Component → Power Up Spot. Type = Magnet.
  3. Drag PowerUp_Magnet onto the marker. Set the child's Position to 0, 0, 0.

The MAGNET pulls coins from up to 11 m for 8 seconds. From the east alley you can pull the two plaza coins without going near the King, if you are fast.

11Start, gate and NavMesh

  1. Select Gameplay/PlayerSpawn. Position 2, 0, -15, Rotation 0, 0, 0 (looks north, to the gate).
  2. Select Static/ExitGate. Position -2, 0, 18, Rotation 0, 0, 0.
  3. Select Static/Walls/GateBlocker. Position -2, 2.5, 19.25. Box Collider Size 8, 5, 1.
  4. Select Static. In NavMesh Surface, click Bake. Look at the blue area: it must reach every coin and the gate.
  5. Save.

The start is at X 2, a bit right of the middle, so the barrier and the plaza pillars do not block the first view.

12LevelDef and Build Profiles

  1. Open Assets/GoldRush/Scripts/Core/Data.cs. Find Levels.All.
  2. Add this line at the end of the list (after the last level you have):
new LevelDef { title = "Night Run", subtitle = "Gold under the moon", seed = 808, scene = "Level8", size = 36, coins = 12, patrollers = 2, chasers = 1, guards = 1, powerUps = 1, enemySpeed = 1.08f, parTime = 100, night = true },
  1. Save the file. Wait for Unity to compile. No red errors.
  2. File → Build Profiles → Scene List: drag in Scenes/Level8. Check it is ticked.

Not sure? Read Practicals, step 6 and 7.

13▶ Play test

  1. Open Level8 and press ▶ Play.
  2. Play the level to the end. Then play it 2 more times and write down your times.
  3. Open MainMenu → Play → LEVELS. Find the NIGHT RUN card.

▶ Play test: check these

  • Switch to night with the moon button in the HUD: the lamps and the King's pad glow, and you can still see the way.
  • Each L pocket has a coin you can reach.
  • The King stays in the plaza. With the MAGNET you can take coins 5 and 6 from outside the pillars.
  • The Ghost does not see the start.
  • Your best time is under the par time (100 s).
  • The intro says NIGHT RUN and "Collect all 12 gold".
  • The gate opens after the last coin, and you win when you run through it.
  • No red errors in the Console.

The card: it shows NIGHT RUN, "Gold under the moon" and "12 GOLD TO FIND". It is locked until you win the level before it. With the updated menu the cards are in two rows; with the old menu it hides under DAILY RUSH (see Practicals, step 8).

Tune the par time: best time + 25 %, rounded to 5 s. Change parTime in Data.cs.

14Make it harder

Make it harder (pick one or two, test after each):
  • A second Ghost at -12, 0, 15.
  • Set enemySpeed = 1.12f (like Gold Fever) and parTime = 85.
  • Move the MAGNET into the SE pocket (and that coin to the alley): you must risk the pocket first.
  • Put a second Mushroom King with its own Home in front of the gate, at -2, 0, 13.

15Your own idea

Now make the level yours. Change at least three things. Write them down first, like a designer: "I want … because …".

Ideas:

  • A theme. A night market (floor pads as stalls, terminals as shops), a mine (long low blocks as rails), a ruined city (stacked blocks: a Block_1x1x1 at Y 2 on a Block_2x2x2).
  • A lamp trail. At night only the lamps light the way. Move the 8 lamps to show the path to the gate, or to mark the risky coins.
  • A second route. A fast, dangerous path past the King and a slow, safe path round the buildings.
  • A secret. A coin in a pocket you can only see from one place.
  • A last chase. After the last coin the monsters get 10 % faster. Put the last coin far from the gate, with the Ghost in between.

Checklist for your own design

  • ☐ My plan is drawn on paper (1 square = 2 m) and the level matches it.
  • ☐ There is a free path, 3 m wide or more, from the start to the gate.
  • ☐ No monster sees the start. Every monster starts 10 m or more away.
  • ☐ Every coin is on the blue NavMesh, and I picked up every coin in a test.
  • ☐ The Mushroom King has a Home, and the circle is not on top of the start or the gate.
  • ☐ 8 lamps or fewer. Nothing floats, nothing sinks into the floor.
  • ☐ coins in Data.cs = the number of CoinSpots. The par time is tuned (best time + 25 %).
  • ☐ A friend played it and I fixed at least one thing they found.
  • ☐ I changed the title and subtitle in Data.cs to fit my idea.

1Tune a level

Goal: Level 1 has 10 coins and 4 slimes.

Changing coins = 6, patrollers = 2 in Data.cs is not enough. Level 1 is the saved scene Level1.unity, and its coins and monsters come from the markers inside it (Part 3, step 1).

Choose one way:

  1. In the scene (recommended): open Level1.unity. Drag 4 more CoinSpot prefabs onto Gameplay/Coins, and 2 more Monster_Slime prefabs onto Gameplay/Monsters. Spread them out. Then change coins = 6 to coins = 10 in Data.cs, so the level card and intro text show the right number.
  2. Generate it: in Data.cs, change the numbers and remove scene = "Level1", from that line. Level 1 is now built by LevelBuilder in Game.unity, with a new layout.

Check: the HUD says 0 / 10 and there are four green slimes.

2A new shop colour

Goal: an “Ocean” colour that costs 40 gold.

  • File: Scripts/Core/Data.cs, array Catalog.Colors.
  • Copy one of the lines and change the id, name, price and colour. The id must be new, for example "color_ocean".
  • Use Gold Rush → Give 500 Gold (debug) to test buying it.

Check: an 8th card appears in the Shop, buying it costs 40, and the hero turns ocean blue. The shop grid has room for 9 colours.

3Your name on the badge

Goal: the monsters wear your name instead of ITSTEP.

  • File: Scripts/Gameplay/Badge.cs. Change DefaultText.
  • Only I, T, S, E, P and space are drawn. For every other letter in your name, add an entry to Glyphs: 7 strings of 5 characters, # for ink and . for empty.
  • Draw the letter on squared paper first. Letters that are missing are skipped without any error.

Check: every monster's chest patch shows your full name.

4Faster ghosts

Goal: make the ghosts scary in Level 2.

  • File: Scripts/Gameplay/EnemyAI.cs, method Configure(), the Chaser line.
  • Try chaseSpeed values of 5, 6.5 and 8. Remember that the hero walks at 5 and sprints at 8.2, and that the level's enemySpeed multiplies your value.
  • Also try detectRange and loseRange.

Think: at which speed does the game stop being fun? Why is it important that the monsters are slower than a sprint?

5A Freeze power-up

Goal: a new power-up, FREEZE, that stops every monster for 4 seconds.

A new power-up touches several files. Do it in this order:

  1. Data.cs: add Freeze to enum PowerType.
  2. PowerUp.cs: add a case PowerType.Freeze to ColorOf (pick an icy blue) and to NameOf ("FREEZE"). Without this, the new power-up looks like a red MAGNET, because default: is the magnet.
  3. GameManager.cs, CollectPowerUp(): add a case. The simplest version stuns every monster: 
    case PowerType.Freeze:
    foreach (var e in enemies) if (e) e.Stun(4f);
    desc = "Monsters frozen!";
    break;
    A stunned monster cannot catch you (Part 3, step 6).
  4. Place it: in Level1.unity, select a PowerUpSpot under Gameplay/PowerUps and set its Type to Freeze.
Extra challenge: make it work like GHOST, with a countdown. Add FreezeDuration, FreezeLeft and FreezeActive next to GhostLeft in GameManager, count it down in Update(), and stop the NavMeshAgent in EnemyAI.Update() while it is active. For a HUD bar, look at UpdatePowerRows() in GameUI.cs and raise the number of rows from 3 to 4.

Check: picking it up shows a FREEZE toast and every monster spins in place with a blue “z”.

6A freeze sound

Goal: FREEZE gets its own icy sound.

  • Follow “Your own sound” in Part 6 to add a Freeze() sound and register it as "freeze".
  • Play it from your new case in CollectPowerUp(): AudioFX.Play("freeze");
  • Experiment: a falling pitch (f0 high, f1 low) sounds “cold”. A rising one sounds like a power-up.

Check: the Console shows no [AudioFX] Unknown sound id warning, and you hear your sound on pickup.

7Level 5

Goal: a fifth campaign level that you can choose in the menu.

  1. In Data.cs, add a fifth LevelDef to Levels.All. Leave out scene, so it is generated (pick your own seed, size, coins and monsters). Saving, stars and unlocking adapt by themselves.
  2. Play Level 4 and win: Level 5 unlocks.
  3. The level-select screen does not fit yet. In GoldRushMenu.BuildLevels(), the card row is sized for exactly 5 cards (4 levels + Daily), so your new card lands on top of the Daily card. Replace the fixed 5 and 4 with values based on Levels.All.Length, and make CardW smaller so 6 cards fit in 1920 pixels.
Want Level 5 as a scene you can edit by hand? After adding it, run Gold Rush → Level Design → Rebuild Level Scenes (overwrites!), then add scene = "Level5" to your LevelDef. Careful: this rebuilds Levels 1–4 too, and any hand edits to them are lost.

Check: six cards in a row, none overlapping, and Level 5 plays.

8Monsters that call for help

Goal: when one monster spots you, nearby monsters join the chase.

  • The spot moment is when a monster enters Alert (look at Go(S.Alert) in EnemyAI.cs).
  • Monsters do not know about each other yet. You can use FindObjectsByType<EnemyAI>(FindObjectsSortMode.None), or keep a static List<EnemyAI> in EnemyAI (add in OnEnable, remove in OnDisable).
  • Add a public method, for example Alarm(Vector3 playerPos). It should only react if the monster is in Patrol, Search or Return, and it must set lastSeen and lastSeenTime before calling Go(S.Alert). Without lastSeenTime, the monster gives up after 2.2 seconds.
  • Only call monsters within, say, 15 m, or the whole level will chase you at once.

Think: a Mushroom King has a leash and goes back home. Should it answer the call? How would you show the player that a monster heard the alarm?

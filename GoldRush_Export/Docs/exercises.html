Exercises

You finished the course. Now make the game your own. Eight short projects, from a five-minute change to a new monster behaviour. Each one lists the files to open and some hints, but not the full answer.

Still in the middle of the course? The Practicals are guided tasks you can do after a group of phases. These exercises are for the end.
Before you startmake a copy of your project folder, so you can always go backStart fromGoldRush_Phase10_Final.unitypackage or your own project

1More gold in Level 1

Goal: Level 1 has 10 coins and 4 slimes.

  1. Open Scenes/Level1.unity.
  2. Add 4 more CoinSpot prefabs to Gameplay/Coins. Spread them out, on the floor (Y = 0) and not inside a block. Each one needs the Coin Spot component and a Coin inside at 0, 1, 0 (Phase 5, step 12 and step 13).
  3. Add 2 more Monster_Slime prefabs to Gameplay/Monsters. Each one must stand on the blue NavMesh (Phase 7, step 13).
  4. In Scripts/Core/Data.cs, change coins = 6 to coins = 10 and patrollers = 2 to patrollers = 4 in the Level 1 line, so the menu card shows the right numbers.
The coins you collect come from the CoinSpots in the scene. Changing only Data.cs is not enough.

Check: the HUD says 0 / 10 and four slimes patrol. Think: is the par time of 40 s still fair? Change parTime if not.

2A new shop colour

Goal: an “Ocean” colour that costs 40 gold.

  • File: Scripts/Core/Data.cs, array Catalog.Colors (Phase 2, step 3).
  • Copy one of the lines and change the id, name, price and colour. The id must be new, for example "color_ocean".
  • Not enough gold to test? Set the price to 1 for a moment, then back to 40. Or use Edit → Clear All PlayerPrefs and play from the start.

Check: a new card appears in the Shop and buying it costs the price. In the final game (phase 10) the hero in the menu turns ocean blue.

3Your name on the badge

Goal: the monsters wear your name instead of ITSTEP.

  • File: Scripts/Gameplay/Badge.cs (Phase 7, step 9). Change DefaultText.
  • Only the letters of ITSTEP (and space) are drawn. For every other letter in your name, add an entry to Glyphs: 7 strings of 5 characters, # for a white pixel and . for background.
  • Draw the letter on squared paper first. A missing letter is skipped without any error.

Check: every monster's chest patch shows your full name.

4Faster ghosts

Goal: make the ghosts scarier.

  • File: Scripts/Gameplay/EnemyAI.cs, method Configure(), the Chaser line. It is the helper you imported in Phase 7, step 7.
  • Try chaseSpeed values of 5, 6.5 and 8. The hero walks at 5 and sprints at 8.2 m/s. The level's enemySpeed in Data.cs multiplies your value.
  • Also try detectRange and loseRange.

Think: at which speed does the game stop being fun? Why is it important that the monsters are slower than a sprint?

5A Freeze power-up

Goal: a new power-up, FREEZE, that stops every monster for 4 seconds.

A new power-up touches several files. Do it in this order:

  1. Data.cs: add Freeze to enum PowerType.
  2. PowerUp.cs: add a case PowerType.Freeze to ColorOf() (pick an icy blue) and to NameOf() ("FREEZE") (Phase 8, step 5). Without this, it looks like a red MAGNET, because default: is the magnet.
  3. GameManager.cs, CollectPowerUp() (Phase 8, step 10): add a case before default:. The simplest version stuns every monster: 
    case PowerType.Freeze:
    foreach (var e in enemies) if (e) e.Stun(4f);
    desc = "Monsters frozen!";
    break;
    A stunned monster spins in place and cannot catch you (Phase 7, step 2).
  4. Make a PowerUp_Freeze prefab: duplicate PowerUp_Ghost and set Type = Freeze (Phase 8, step 6). Put one in Gameplay/PowerUps in Level 1, inside a marker with a Power Up Spot of the same type (Phase 8, step 8).
Extra challenge: make it work like GHOST, with a countdown bar. Look at how GhostLeft, GhostDuration and GhostActive work in GameManager, and copy the idea.

Check: you see a FREEZE toast on the HUD on pickup (Phase 9) and every monster spins in place with a blue “z”.

6A freeze sound

Goal: FREEZE gets its own icy sound.

  • Make or find a short .wav file and name it freeze.wav. A free tool such as Audacity or an online “sfxr” generator works well. A falling pitch sounds “cold”.
  • Put it in Art/Audio/ and add it to the Sounds list of GoldRushAssets. The file name is the sound id (Phase 1, step 7).
  • Play it from your new case in CollectPowerUp(): AudioFX.Play("freeze");

Check: the Console shows no [AudioFX] Unknown sound id warning, and you hear your sound on pickup.

7Level 5

Goal: a fifth level that you can choose in the menu.

  1. Open Level4.unity and use File → Save As → Level5.unity. Rename the level root and change the layout, coins and monsters. Levels 2–4 come in Phase 10; build your layout from the kit like in Phase 3.
  2. In Data.cs (Phase 2, step 3), add a fifth LevelDef to Levels.All with scene = "Level5", a title, the number of coins and a par time. Saving, stars and unlocking adapt by themselves.
  3. Add Level5 to File → Build Profiles → Scene List (Phase 3, step 17).
  4. The level-select screen does not fit yet. In GoldRushMenu.cs, method BuildLevels(), the cards are placed for exactly 4 levels + the Daily card, so your new card lands on top of the Daily card. Use Levels.All.Length instead of the fixed numbers, make CardW smaller, and make the Cards object in the MainMenu scene wider (Phase 2, step 8).

Check: win Level 4 and Level 5 unlocks. Six cards in a row, none overlapping, and Level 5 plays.

8Monsters that call for help

Goal: when one monster spots you, nearby monsters join the chase.

  • The spot moment is when a monster enters Alert (look for Go(S.Alert) in EnemyAI.cs).
  • Monsters do not know about each other yet. Keep a static List<EnemyAI> in EnemyAI (add in OnEnable, remove in OnDisable).
  • Add a public method, for example Alarm(Vector3 playerPos). It should only react if the monster is in Patrol, Search or Return, and it must set lastSeen and lastSeenTime before it calls Go(S.Alert). Without lastSeenTime, the monster gives up after 2.2 seconds.
  • Only call monsters within, say, 15 m (use sqrMagnitude), or the whole level chases you at once.

Think: the Mushroom King guards its home and goes back after 13 m. Should it answer the call? How would you show the player that a monster heard the alarm?

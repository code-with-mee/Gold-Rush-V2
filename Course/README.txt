GOLD RUSH – Unity 6 course materials
====================================

1. Open START_HERE.html (or Docs/index.html) in a web browser. It works offline.
2. Install Unity 6000.6.10f1 or newer, then in Unity Hub choose
   Add > Add project from disk and pick the GoldRushDemo folder.
3. In Unity, use the menu Gold Rush > Play.
   If anything looks pink or broken, run Gold Rush > Setup Project first.
4. Follow the guides in order:

   Part 1  The arena and a walking hero    Docs/part-1-guide.html
   Part 2  Characters and animation        Docs/part-2-guide.html
   Part 3  Gold and monsters               Docs/part-3-guide.html
   Part 4  Rules, menu, win and lose       Docs/part-4-guide.html
   Part 5  Mobile controls                 Docs/part-5-guide.html
   Part 6  Day, night, sound and effects   Docs/part-6-guide.html
   Exercises                               Docs/exercises.html

   Every guide is also a printable PDF next to its HTML page.

Help: Docs/troubleshooting.html and Docs/glossary.html
